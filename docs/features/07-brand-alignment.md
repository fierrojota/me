# Brand alignment

> The site now speaks one language, Spanish, and says who the author is: an engineer who puts process into everything, code and life alike.

**Added:** 2026-10-09
**Slug:** 07-brand-alignment

---

## What it does

The homepage is now Spanish only, with copy that states the author's positioning and the three themes behind his work: engineering and career, life under construction, and a library of books and anime. The language selector is gone from the header, and the contact section no longer advertises engineering work for hire. A link to the YouTube channel now sits in the footer.

## Who it's for

Spanish-speaking engineers, tech leads and architects who find the author through LinkedIn, GitHub or YouTube and want to know who is behind the work. No login needed.

## Capabilities

- **One language**: you always see the site in Spanish, whatever your browser or an earlier visit set.
- **No language picker**: the header shows only the navigation, on desktop and in the mobile menu.
- **Clear positioning**: the first screen says "El ingeniero que le pone proceso a todo — al código y a la vida."
- **Who is behind it**: the About section shows the author as a senior backend engineer, husband, father of two and basketball player, plus the three themes of his work.
- **A plain way to get in touch**: the contact section invites questions, ideas and comments about what he publishes, with the email button.
- **Find the channel**: the footer links to the YouTube channel, opening in a new tab.
- **Consistent everywhere**: the page title, description, install name and the file for AI assistants all use the same Spanish positioning.
- **Errors in Spanish**: if something breaks, the fallback message is also in Spanish.
